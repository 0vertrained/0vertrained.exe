Privacy Policy

0VERTRAINED — Last updated: October 22, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how 0VERTRAINED ("0VERTRAINED," "we," "us," or "the App") collects, uses, discloses, stores, and protects information when you use the App, connect supported wearables, use AI coaching, enable notifications, or participate in opt-in social features.

This policy is written for the current app architecture. It is not a substitute for legal advice, and the policy should be reviewed by counsel before a commercial launch or expansion into new jurisdictions.

1. Who controls your information

0VERTRAINED is operated by the developer reachable at 0vertrained.exe@gmail.com. For privacy requests, security questions, or deletion requests, contact that address.

2. Information we collect

Account and authentication

  • Username and account identifiers.
  • Password credentials managed through Supabase authentication.
  • Recovery phrase hash. The recovery phrase itself is not stored in plain text.
  • Signup status and account metadata needed to operate the limited beta.

Training, recovery, nutrition, and program data

  • Workout logs, exercises, sets, reps, weight, RIR, cardio distance, pace, workout completion, session notes, and training load calculations.
  • Recovery check-ins such as sleep duration, energy, soreness, stress, body weight, supplements, and wellness notes you enter.
  • Nutrition entries, meal plan preferences, calories, protein, carbohydrates, fats, water, and related fuel targets.
  • Program Builder choices, generated programs, schedule, training goals, equipment, experience level, session length, cardio preference, deload settings, and edits.

Connected wearable data

When you choose to connect a supported wearable, the App receives only the data you authorize through that provider's OAuth or consent flow.

  • Oura: sleep, readiness, activity, heart-rate, HRV, resting heart rate, SpO2, body temperature deviation, respiratory rate, tags, sessions, workout summaries, and related metadata available through the authorized Oura API scopes.
  • Whoop: recovery, sleep, cycle, workout, and body-measurement records available through the authorized Whoop API scopes.
  • Google Health / Fitbit by Google: sleep, HRV, resting heart rate, respiratory rate, oxygen saturation, steps, active minutes, active zone minutes, calories, exercise summaries, profile/settings metadata, and other data you authorize through Google Health scopes. Google Health/Fitbit data is used to provide user-facing recovery, readiness, activity, and optional AI Coach features you enable.
  • Strava: activity summaries such as runs, rides, walks, hikes, GPS workout metadata, distance, duration, elevation, pace, heart-rate fields when authorized, and related athlete/profile identifiers. Strava data is used for user-visible activity import/display, sync status, and deduplication. Strava data is excluded from AI Coach and is not used to train, fine-tune, evaluate, or improve AI models.
  • OAuth access and refresh tokens needed to sync provider data. Tokens are encrypted server-side and are not exposed to the browser.
  • Wearable sync event metadata, such as source, event type, status, counts, days covered, and timestamps. Sync events do not include raw payloads or tokens.

AI Coach and Form Check

  • When you use AI Coach, the App sends a compact, sanitized training/recovery context and your coach message to the server-side AI provider. We do not include OAuth tokens, passwords, recovery phrases, service keys, or unnecessary account identifiers.
  • Google Health/Fitbit data is included in AI Coach context only when you connect Google Health/Fitbit and enable the relevant coaching features. Strava-sourced data is excluded from AI Coach by default.
  • Coach usage counts may be stored to enforce daily or monthly beta limits.
  • Form Check may process a lift video to generate movement analysis, but the App stores derived summaries such as depth, tempo, bar path, capture quality, form score, coaching cues, and confidence. Raw videos are not stored by the App.

Notifications, social features, diagnostics, and device storage

  • Push notification subscription data, including endpoint, p256dh key, auth key, reminder preference, and timezone offset, if you enable browser notifications.
  • Opt-in social-circle, leaderboard, and PR-board data such as username/handle, circle membership, invite code, aggregate stats, and PR records. Raw daily entries remain private.
  • Client error reports such as error message, stack trace, component stack, source, path, app version, user agent, and timestamp.
  • First-party product events tied to your account, such as which first-run screens you saw, which first-step buttons you tapped, whether a wearable connected, and when your first readiness verdict appeared, each with a timestamp. These are stored in our own database, are not shared with analytics or advertising services, and are used only to understand whether the App works for new users.
  • Browser localStorage data used for offline-first performance, including recent entries, program/settings cache, coach conversation history, and local UI preferences.

  • Provide the App, authenticate accounts, sync data, and keep your account separate from other users.
  • Display dashboards for Today, Recovery, Fuel, Progress, Profile, Form Check, and related app views.
  • Generate readiness, recovery, load, nutrition, program, trend, and coaching insights.
  • Operate optional wearable sync, optional push notifications, optional AI Coach, and optional social features.
  • Debug crashes, prevent abuse, enforce beta limits, maintain security, and improve reliability.
  • Respond to privacy, support, deletion, export, or security requests.
  • Comply with applicable law, including data-security and breach-notification obligations.

5. How we share information

We do not sell personal information or personal health data. We share information only as needed to operate the App, at your direction, or as legally required.

  • Supabase: authentication, database, row-level security, and backend storage.
  • Netlify: hosting, serverless functions, wearable sync endpoints, notification endpoints, and API routing.
  • Oura, Whoop, Google Health/Fitbit, and Strava: authorization and data syncing only when you connect those services. Their own terms, privacy policies, API rules, and account controls apply to their services.
  • Google API data: Google Health/Fitbit data is used only to provide and improve user-facing 0VERTRAINED features you authorize. We do not sell Google user data, use it for advertising, or use it to train generalized AI or machine-learning models. 0VERTRAINED's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
  • Strava API data: Strava data is displayed back to the connected user and used for activity import/sync features. We do not disclose Strava-sourced activity data to other users through public feeds, leaderboards, or AI Coach by default.
  • Ollama Cloud: server-side AI Coach processing when you use the coach feature. The App's server sends a sanitized coaching context and your coach message to models hosted by Ollama Cloud.
  • Browser push services: push notifications are routed through browser/platform notification infrastructure if you enable notifications.
  • Other users: only for opt-in social features, where visible data is limited to handle-level aggregate stats, PR records, circle membership, and information you choose to share.
  • Legal and safety: if necessary to comply with law, respond to lawful requests, protect rights and safety, investigate abuse, or enforce the Terms.

6. Cookies, tracking, ads, and Global Privacy Control

The App does not use third-party advertising cookies, advertising SDKs, or analytics trackers. Because we do not sell personal information, share it for cross-context behavioral advertising, or use targeted advertising, we do not process opt-out preference signals such as Global Privacy Control for those purposes. If this changes, this policy will be updated before the change is implemented.

7. Storage, security, and local device data

  • Data in transit is encrypted via HTTPS/TLS.
  • Supabase Row Level Security is used so authenticated users can access only their own owner-scoped rows, except for explicitly opt-in aggregate social features.
  • Wearable OAuth tokens are stored server-side, encrypted at rest, and are not returned to browser clients.
  • Service role keys, OAuth client secrets, and AI provider keys are server-side environment variables and should not be committed to source control.
  • Some data is cached in browser localStorage for offline-first use. Anyone with access to your unlocked device/browser profile may be able to view local cached data.
  • No security system is perfect. Use a strong password, protect your device, and contact us immediately if you suspect unauthorized access.

8. Data retention and deletion

  • Account, training, nutrition, recovery, program, and settings data are retained while your account is active unless you delete or request deletion.
  • Disconnecting Oura, Whoop, Google Health/Fitbit, or Strava deletes provider tokens and provider-derived data stored by the App for that source from active app tables, subject to backups, audit logs, legal obligations, and fraud/security retention.
  • Push subscriptions are retained while reminders are enabled and are removed when you unsubscribe or delete your account.
  • Client error reports, product events, and coach usage counters are retained only as long as reasonably needed for reliability, abuse prevention, and beta operations.
  • Deletion requests will be completed within 30 days unless a shorter period is legally required or retention is necessary for security, fraud prevention, legal compliance, or dispute resolution.

To request deletion or export, email 0vertrained.exe@gmail.com.

8A. Connected-service controls

You can disconnect supported providers in Settings → Integrations. You may also revoke access directly inside the connected provider account, such as your Google Account permissions page or Strava account settings. Revoking access at the provider may stop future sync before 0VERTRAINED receives a disconnect event.

Provider APIs can change, fail, revoke tokens, enforce rate limits, or return incomplete data. 0VERTRAINED stores the scopes granted, sync status, provider account identifiers, and normalized records needed to show connection state and imported metrics.

Some U.S. laws, including the FTC Health Breach Notification Rule and state consumer health data laws, may apply to certain health-app data practices. If we discover a breach of unsecured identifiable health information requiring notice, we will notify affected users, regulators, and others as required by applicable law.

11. Children

The App is not directed to children under 16, and we do not knowingly collect information from children under 16. If you believe a child has created an account or connected a wearable, contact us so we can delete the information.
